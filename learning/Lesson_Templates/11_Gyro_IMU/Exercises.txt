------------------------------------------------------
EXERCISE 1 — Degrees and Radians  (★★☆☆)
------------------------------------------------------
Display heading in both degrees AND radians simultaneously.
Verify that they agree: 90° should show ~1.571 rad.

------------------------------------------------------
EXERCISE 2 — Motor Stops at Zero  (★★★☆)
------------------------------------------------------
Add a motor. Make it:
  - Run at 0.5 when heading > 0  (robot turned right)
  - Run at -0.5 when heading < 0 (robot turned left)
  - Stop when heading is within 5 degrees of 0

This mimics a basic "return to center" behavior.

------------------------------------------------------
EXERCISE 3 — Orientation Experiment  (★★★☆)
------------------------------------------------------
Change the LogoFacingDirection from UP to FORWARD.
Does the heading still work correctly?
What happens to pitch and roll?

This helps you understand why hub orientation matters on a real robot.

------------------------------------------------------
EXERCISE 4 — CHALLENGE: Heading to Servo  (★★★★)
------------------------------------------------------
Map the heading (−180 to +180 degrees) to a servo position (0.0 to 1.0).
Use Range.scale() to do the conversion:
  double servoPos = Range.scale(headingDegrees, -180, 180, 0.0, 1.0);

Set a servo to that position. When you rotate the hub, the servo should
track the heading. This is similar to how a compass needle works!
