EXERCISE 3 — Field-Relative Driving  (★★★★)
------------------------------------------------------
Implement the field-relative driving pattern from the book
(Listing 20.6). You'll need your IMU from Lesson 11.

The polar-rotation steps:
  1. Convert (forward, right) to polar (r, theta)
  2. Subtract the robot's IMU heading from theta
  3. Convert back to Cartesian (newForward, newRight)
  4. Call drive(newForward, newRight, rotate)

Code:
  double robotAngle = imu.getRobotYawPitchRollAngles().getYaw(AngleUnit.RADIANS);
  double theta = Math.atan2(forward, right);
  double r     = Math.hypot(forward, right);
  theta = AngleUnit.normalizeRadians(theta - robotAngle);
  double newForward = r * Math.sin(theta);
  double newRight   = r * Math.cos(theta);
